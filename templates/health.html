Recovery

  1. Turso unreachable: reads keep serving from the local replica and writes show the offline page. The collector spools punches and replays them in order once sync returns.
  2. Dead-lettered punches stay in the spool's dead table; nothing is dropped.
  3. Data loss on the primary: restore from Turso point-in-time recovery first, then from the latest nightly export in R2.

The full runbook is in the README, section "Recovery".
