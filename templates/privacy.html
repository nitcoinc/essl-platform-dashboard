How we use attendance data

Attendance reminders help catch swipes the door reader missed, so your hours are right.

What we use

Only your swipe times and which door device recorded them. Nothing else about you.

What we work out

Your own typical day, such as when you usually leave and how long your breaks usually are. It is recomputed every week from your recent swipes, so it follows changes in your routine.

Why

To notice when a swipe looks missed and remind you, so your attendance is correct. Reminders assume the device missed a swipe. They are never used for discipline.

Who sees it

You and HR. Your manager sees your hours as before and gets a copy when you report a problem, but never sees your typical pattern.

How long we keep it

Reminder records are deleted after 90 days.

How to correct something

Use the link in a reminder email, or sign in and choose Report a problem on a day from the last 7 days. HR reviews each change; your device records are never edited.
